## Observation

- Error decreases at each tested sample budget for each of the four probe seeds, for
  both parameter groups. Mean relative error at S=1024 is 4.084% and 4.145%.
- The predeclared study gates passed for both parameter groups: mean relative error
  at S=1024 < 0.15, and < 0.25 times the mean error at S=4. Individual-draw
  monotonicity is not an acceptance criterion.
- Focused suite: **19 passed, 6 skipped**, in 9.12 s. Full suite: **26 passed,
  19 skipped**, in 6.88 s (one CPU thread for the full suite). Six remaining
  second-order skips are W4 signal tests; the other skips belong to unfinished
  benchmark/D1/D3/gradient-inconsistency work.
- The tests cover a CUDA real-renderer smoke check and exact analytical CUDA
  normalization; the convergence table above is a CPU result.
- S=1024 estimator timings on this tiny CPU scene range from approximately 1.63 to
  1.78 s for position and 1.84 to 1.89 s for position/log-scale. Timings are
  diagnostic, include ordinary process/allocator effects, and are not a controlled
  GPU/production benchmark. The first S=4 timing includes noticeable startup cost.

## Interpretation

The implementation estimates the declared per-Gaussian rendered-image information
blocks, with observed error reduction compatible with the expected Monte Carlo
`1/sqrt(S)` trend. The analytical test independently verifies sample averaging,
view summation, scalar parameter handling, parameter ordering, and unused blocks.
The exact-reference comparison tests the production PyTorch rasterizer derivatives.

This satisfies the numerical estimator objective for W3 on the tested setup. It does
not establish that any derived reliability score identifies geometric failures or
produces faithful interventions; those require the later D1/D2/D3 experiments.

## Limitations

- One constructed scene and two views; no held-out prediction or failure labels.
- S=4 has high variance here. The default is an inexpensive starting setting, not a
  demonstrated production-quality choice. Evaluate S/damping/ranking stability in W4.
- The 6x6 block uses **log-scale**, not activated scale. The PUP-style wrapper must
  document its parameter basis and any chain-rule conversion before damping.
- Cache assumes an immutable context. Use a fresh context after any model, camera,
  background, or backend change; do not modify a returned cached tensor.
- No full-scene or GTX 1650 profiling; required checkpoints are absent locally.
- CUDA random streams differ from CPU; cross-device bit-identical estimates are not
  claimed. gsplat is not validated by this experiment.
- Source changes remain uncommitted. `environment.json` records the base commit and
  dirty state, not a claim that the estimator exists in the base commit.
